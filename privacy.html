Privacy Policy for PranaSphere

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Local-First Data Storage

PranaSphere is designed with privacy first. All profile names, 7-point check-in responses, Guna ratios (Sattva, Rajas, Tamas), and 7-day trend history are stored locally on your device via browser storage (LocalStorage). We do not transmit or sell your personal mood check-ins to external servers.

2. Wellness & Educational Disclaimer

PranaSphere is a mindfulness, self-reflection, and lifestyle awareness tool inspired by traditional Ayurvedic philosophy. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any physical or mental health condition.

3. Audio & Sharing Permissions

Soundscapes are synthesized locally on your device using the Web Audio API. When you use the "Share Aura" feature, an image is generated locally on your device and shared only with the app you explicitly select.

4. Contact

For questions regarding this policy or app support, contact: support@pranasphere.app
